"Sovereign of the Day of Recompense."

Allah is the sole Owner and King of the Day of Judgment, the day every soul is repaid for what it did. In this world people claim kingship and authority, but on that Day no one will speak or intercede except by His permission. Mentioning the Day of Recompense immediately after mercy balances hope with accountability: the believer lives between hope in Allah's mercy and awareness of standing before Him. Reciting this daily in salah keeps the reality of the Hereafter present in the heart.
