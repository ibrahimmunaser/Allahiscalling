"The Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."

The two names of mercy are repeated after mentioning Allah's lordship over the worlds. Scholars explain the pairing: His lordship is not one of harshness but of mercy — He is a Master who is merciful to His servants. The repetition between verse 1 and verse 3 emphasizes that mercy is the dominant attribute through which Allah relates to creation, and it prepares the heart for the next verse about the Day of Judgment: the Judge the believer will meet is, above all, merciful.
