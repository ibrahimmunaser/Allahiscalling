"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."

The Basmalah opens the Quran and nearly every surah. The believer begins recitation — and every important act — in the name of Allah, seeking His help and blessing. Ar-Rahman (the Entirely Merciful) describes Allah's vast mercy encompassing all creation, while Ar-Raheem (the Especially Merciful) describes His particular, continuing mercy toward the believers. Scholars note that opening with these two names sets the tone of the entire Quran: a message from a Lord whose mercy precedes His punishment.
