"Guide us to the straight path."

After affirming worship and reliance, the servant makes the most important supplication possible: guidance to the straight path — the clear way of truth, Islam, with no crookedness. Scholars note that even the guided are commanded to ask for guidance, because guidance has levels: to be shown the path, to be kept firm upon it, and to be increased in it. Asking in the plural ("guide us") reminds the believer that they walk this path as part of a community, praying for one another.
