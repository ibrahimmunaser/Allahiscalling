"All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

Alhamdulillah combines praise and gratitude: every perfect quality and every blessing belongs to Allah alone. "Lord of the worlds" (Rabb al-'alamin) affirms that He creates, owns, sustains and manages everything that exists — mankind, jinn, angels, and all creation. Ibn Kathir explains that "Rabb" implies complete ownership and care: He does not merely create and leave, but continuously nurtures His creation. The verse teaches the believer to attribute all good in their life to its true source.
