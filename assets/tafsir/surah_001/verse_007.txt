"The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have earned [Your] anger or of those who are astray."

The straight path is defined by the people who walked it: the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs and the righteous whom Allah favored (see Quran 4:69). The verse then asks protection from two deviations: those who knew the truth but did not act on it (earning anger), and those who acted without knowledge (going astray). Together the surah closes as a complete supplication — praise, then worship, then the request that matters most — which is why it is recited in every unit of every prayer.
