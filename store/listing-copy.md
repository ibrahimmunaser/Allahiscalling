## Full description
Allah Invites You to Salah turns each prayer time into a clear, conscious moment.

When a prayer enters, you get a call-style reminder with two choices: Answer or Decline. The goal is simple — make salah feel personal, honorable, and hard to ignore, without guilt-heavy pressure.

Features:
• Accurate prayer times based on your location or chosen city
• Call-style reminders when each salah enters
• Answer to pray now, or Decline to be gently reminded again
• Works offline for city search from a bundled database
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts

Prayer times are estimates. Please verify with your local masjid.
